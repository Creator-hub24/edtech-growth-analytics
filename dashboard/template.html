Learner Growth Dashboard

REAL DATAOpen University Learning Analytics Dataset · 32,593 enrolments · 7 modules · 4 presentations (2013–2014)

Key takeaways

    Learner funnel

    Registered → used the learning platform (VLE) → submitted a first assessment → passed. Most learners get started; the big loss comes after the first assessment.

    When do learners drop out?

    Share of all withdrawals by timing. About a quarter quit before the course even starts, and half are gone by day 30.

    Retention curves by presentation

    % of registered learners still enrolled on each day since the module start. B = February start, J = October start. The start month makes little difference; 2014 retained worse than 2013.

    Early engagement (“magic moment”)

    Distinct days active on the platform in the first 14 days vs outcome (learners still enrolled at day 14). No sharp cliff: every extra day helps, and the biggest jump is from 0 to 1 day.

    First assessment score

    Pass rate by score on the first assessment submitted. Crossing 60 is the sharpest threshold in the data.

    Learner segments

    Pass and withdrawal rate by segment. IMD = deprivation band (0–10% = most deprived area).

    HYPOTHETICALUnit economics scenario

    Not real data. OULAD has no prices, revenue or marketing spend. This uses made-up assumptions (₹2,500 fee per module, refund if withdrawn by day 14, 70% margin, 45%/10% re-enrolment, flat ₹750 acquisition cost). Only the pass and withdrawal behaviour per band is real.
